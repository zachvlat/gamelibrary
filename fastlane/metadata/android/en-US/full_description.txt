GameShelf aggregates your owned game libraries from Epic Games Store, GOG, Amazon Gaming, Steam and itch.io into one place.

Log in once via in-app WebView OAuth, browse all your games offline thanks to local caching with 7-day TTL. Optional IGDB enrichment adds covers, descriptions and genres.

Built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 dynamic colors. No ads, no tracking, fully open source.

Note: this app connects to proprietary store services (Epic, GOG, Amazon, Steam, itch.io, IGDB) and requires accounts on those services to sync libraries.
